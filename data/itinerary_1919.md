# Bernard Berenson — Reconstructed Itinerary, 1919

Reconstructed from the two 1919 pocket diaries (Jan–Jun and Jul–Dec). Locations are
inferred from block-capital place names, hotel names, travel verbs, and—during settled
periods—from recurring local addresses and dense social/diplomatic appointments. Spread
numbers (sNNN) refer to the transcription digest.

---

## BOOK 1 — January to June 1919

### Paris (1 Jan – 4 Mar) — France · spreads s001–s016
Berenson is resident in Paris throughout the Peace Conference winter. The diary is a
continuous stream of lunches, teas, dinners and visits, with recurring Paris addresses
(Rue Spontini/Passy, Rue de Lubeck, Quai de Bourbon, Bd Lannes, Bd des Invalides,
Ave. Malakoff, Rue d'Anjou) and repeated Sunday excursions to Versailles.
- Diplomats / public figures: **Venizelos, Isvolsky, Albert Thomas, Lippmann,
  Lindsay–Pershing, Vandervelde, Bakhmeteff, Rohan-Chabot, La Rochefoucauld**.
- Society / art world: **Lady Hood, Cole Porter, Polignac, Seligmann, Orpen, Cuevas de
  Vera, Mrs J. Harriman**.

### French Riviera (5 Mar – 25 Mar) — France · spreads s017–s022(L)
- **5 Mar:** Leaves Paris 8.20 for **Beaulieu / Cap Ferrat**, Hotel Cap Ferrat.
- Excursions to **Monte Carlo**; stays at Hotel des Pins; tea at "Maryland" with the
  **Duke of Connaught**; days of rest ("Bad").
- **25 Mar:** Mary Berenson leaves for Florence.

### Hyères (26 Mar – 12 Apr) — France · spreads s022(R)–s026
- **26 Mar:** Leaves for **Hyères, Hotel du Parc**, with **Edith Wharton**.
- Excursions around Toulon/Hyères (Les Salettes "classical landscape", Sohier villa,
  an abbey); **Norton** joins, then Edie & Norton leave; **Ojetti, Bourget**.
- Reading and writing. Prepares to return north.

### Paris (13 Apr – 2 May) — France · spreads s027–s031
- **13 Apr:** leaves Hyères for Paris **via Toulon**; **14 Apr: PARIS**.
- Resumes Peace-Conference life: **Vandervelde, S. & A. Reinach, Gauvain, Savinkov,
  Beneš, Mme Venizelos, Tyler, Isvolsky, Morgenthau, Tyrrell, Mallet, Frankfurter**;
  Italian Exhibition; **Petit Palais**.
- **2 May:** LEAVE at 7.15 (for Spain).

### SPAIN — Art tour begins

#### Barcelona & Catalonia (3 May – 14 May) — spreads s032–s035(L)
- **3 May:** arrive **Barcelona** 8 p.m., **Hotel Bristol**.
- Cathedral, archives, museums, public buildings; collections (Grases Hernández);
  **Murillo**.
- Day trips: **Vic** (diary "VICH"), **Terrassa** ("Tarrasa/Tarrassa"), **Pedralbes**,
  Museo del Parque, **Manresa**, **Sitges**.
- **13 May:** to **Tarragona**.

#### Tarragona → Valencia → Alicante (15 May – 18 May) — spreads s035–s036(L)
- **15 May:** Tarragona, Hotel Paris.
- **16 May:** to **Valencia**, Palace Hotel; all-day touring.
- **18 May:** to **Alicante**.

#### Alicante → Murcia → Granada (19 May – 28 May) — spreads s036–s038(L)
- **19 May:** Alicante (Hotel Victoria) **via Elche to Murcia**.
- **21 May:** Murcia **via Lorca, Baza, Guadix to Granada**.
- **22–28 May:** **Granada** — Alhambra, Cathedral, Capilla Real, Cartuja, Generalife;
  lunch Mersmann, the Marqués, Duquesa de San Pedro.

#### Seville & Córdoba (29 May – 3 Jun) — spreads s038(R)–s039
- **29 May:** Granada **to Seville**; 31 May – 1 Jun **Seville**.
- **2–3 Jun:** **Córdoba** — San Jerónimo, **Medina Azahara** (diary "Madinat az-Zahra").

#### Madrid & central Spain (4 Jun – 24 Jun) — spreads s040–s045(L)
- **4 Jun:** **Madrid, Ritz**. Base for the next three weeks.
- Scholars/society: **Gómez-Moreno, Allende-Salazar, Duke of Alba, Pío (Lagasca 77),
  Metternich, Villaurrutia, Duque del Infantado, Lázaro**; repeated **Prado** visits.
- Day / short trips: **Ávila** with Russell (9 Jun), **El Escorial** (13 Jun),
  **Toledo** (15–16 Jun).

#### Northwest circuit — Salamanca to Galicia (25 Jun – 1 Jul) — spreads s045–s046
- **25 Jun:** leave Madrid by auto for **Ávila & Salamanca**.
- **26 Jun:** Salamanca; **27 Jun:** **Zamora, Astorga**.
- **28 Jun:** Astorga, **Lugo**; **29 Jun:** **Santiago de Compostela** (Hotel Suizo).
- **1 Jul:** **A Coruña**, **Ribadeo**.

## BOOK 2 — July to December 1919

### Spain, northern coast & Camino (2 Jul – 16 Jul) — spreads s001(L)–s004
- **2 Jul:** Ribadeo to **Oviedo** (Hotel Covadonga).
- **4 Jul:** via the **Pajares pass** to **León** (4–5 Jul).
- **6 Jul:** León, **San Miguel de Escalada, Sahagún, Palencia**; 7 Jul Palencia, Frómista.
- **8–9 Jul:** **Burgos** (Hotel Norte).
- **10 Jul:** Santo Domingo de la Calzada, San Millán, Nájera.
- **11 Jul:** Logroño, Viana, Estella, **Pamplona** (diary "PAMPELVNA").
- **12 Jul:** Pamplona, Sangüesa, **Javier** (diary "XAVIER").
- **13–16 Jul:** **San Sebastián** — Fuenterrabía, audience with **Queen Cristina**.
- **16 Jul:** leave for Paris.

### Paris & northern-France art tours (17 Jul – 7 Oct) — France · spreads s004(R)–s024
Berenson is again based in Paris, interspersed with Gothic/Romanesque touring expeditions,
several made with the medievalist **Arthur Kingsley Porter**.
- **17 Jul:** **PARIS**; renews passport; Lazzaroni, Curtis; Mary leaves 20 Jul.
- **22–25 Jul (Senlis tour):** **Senlis**, Clermont, Beauvais, Raray, Chaalis, St-Leu,
  Beaumont, with Kingsley Porter.
- **27 Jul:** day excursion **Rampillon, St-Loup-de-Naud, Provins**.
- **30 Jul – 6 Aug:** **Versailles** (dinners with Hyde).
- **7–10 Aug:** **St-Brice-sous-Forêt** (Edith Wharton's Pavillon Colombe).
- **11–13 Aug:** Paris.
- **14–24 Aug (Burgundy/Jura Romanesque tour)** with Kingsley Porter: Sens,
  Villeneuve-sur-Yonne, **Pontigny**, Fontenay, **Dijon**, Dole, Chissey, Arbois, Poligny,
  Baume-les-Messieurs, Bourg-en-Bresse, St-Paul-de-Varax, **Tournus**, Berzé-la-Ville,
  **Cluny**, Bois-Ste-Marie, **Charlieu**, Montceaux-l'Étoile, Paray-le-Monial, Perrecy,
  **Autun**, Saulieu, Avallon, **Vézelay**, Vermenton, **Auxerre** — back to **Paris 24 Aug**.
- **25 Aug – 7 Sep:** Paris (Mrs Harriman, Royall Tyler, Venizelos, Balfour,
  Carl Hamilton, Willy Buckler, Prince Paul); some days at Edith Wharton's.
- **10–11 Sep:** **Chartres**.
- **12–15 Sep:** Paris; Mary arrives.
- **16–19 Sep:** Mary leaves for Florence; BB to **Amiens** (cathedral).
- **20–26 Sep:** Edith Wharton's, then Paris.
- **27–28 Sep:** **Bourges** (cathedral).
- **29 Sep – 7 Oct:** Paris (Joe Duveen, Carl Hamilton, Tyler, R. W. Bliss,
  Elsie de Wolfe).
- **7 Oct:** LEAVE Paris for Italy.

### Italy — Villa I Tatti, Settignano (8 Oct – 31 Dec) — spreads s025–s046
- **8 Oct:** Mary meets him at **Pisa**; on to **Settignano** (Villa I Tatti, near Florence).
  This is home for the rest of the year.
- **12 Oct:** excursion to **Monte Senario**.
- **17–21 Oct (Umbria tour):** Borgo San Sepolcro, **Perugia**, **Assisi**, Spello,
  Foligno, Trevi, **Arezzo**, then HOME.
- **Rest of October–December:** settled at I Tatti — reading and art-historical study
  (Venturi's *Storia dell'Arte*, Bédier, Henry Adams' *Mont-St-Michel & Chartres*, Mâle,
  van Marle, Schlumberger, the *Très Riches Heures*); steady stream of Florentine and
  expatriate visitors: **Mrs Ross, Loeser, Salvemini, Carlo Placci, Cecil, Geoffrey,
  Lady Annabel O'Neil, the Bussys, Acton, Santayana, Davidsohn**.
- **19–21 Nov:** study of Sienese painters (Barna, the Lorenzettis) — probably short
  study trips to/around **Siena**; Villa Medici.
- **24 Dec:** the Gibsons leave.
- **25–31 Dec:** ill in bed with fever and cold over Christmas; recovering by year's end.

## Uncertainties & corrections

**Place corrections made (with reason):**
- **GRAADA → Granada** (s038): slip omitting the N.
- **VICH → Vic** (s033): old Catalan spelling; the town of Vic, Barcelona province.
- **Tarrasa / Tarrassa → Terrassa** (s033, s034): Castilian spelling of Catalan Terrassa.
- **PAMPELVNA → Pamplona** (s003, s004): Latinate uppercase form.
- **XAVIER → Javier** (s004): Castillo de Javier, Navarre.
- **Madinat az-Zahra → Medina Azahara** (s039): caliphal city near Córdoba (same site).
- **Sergé-la-Ville → Berzé-la-Ville** (s013): the famous Romanesque chapel near Cluny;
  the surrounding towns (Tournus, Cluny, Bois-Ste-Marie) confirm the Mâconnais setting.

**Spreads where the primary location is genuinely uncertain:**
- **s017–s022 (5–25 Mar, Riviera):** Confidently the French Riviera, but the exact base
  oscillates between Beaulieu / Cap Ferrat and nearby resorts (Monte Carlo, "Maryland",
  Hotel des Pins). Pinned to **Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat / Beaulieu** as the most-cited base.
- **s023–s026 (28 Mar–12 Apr, Hyères):** Based at Hyères with Edith Wharton, but
  "Bourges" on 28 Mar (s023) is geographically impossible there and is almost certainly a
  misread local name (a Provençal village or "Bormes"?) — left as Hyères. Low-medium.
- **s036 of Book 2 (19–22 Nov):** "Barna / week in quarries / the Lorenzettis" strongly
  suggests Sienese-school study, plausibly day trips to **Siena**, but he may equally have
  been studying photographs/collections at I Tatti. Marked **low confidence**.
- **s042 of Book 2 (13–16 Dec):** "To London" on 14 Dec is isolated and immediately
  followed by Florentine entries (Tosca; Santayana & Mrs Ross on 16 Dec), so it is read as
  a book/note rather than an actual trip to London. Kept as Settignano.
- Numerous **personal names** remain uncertain ([?]) but do not affect the geographic
  reconstruction.
