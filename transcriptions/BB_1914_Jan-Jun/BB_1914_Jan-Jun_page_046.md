# BB_1914_Jan-Jun — page 46 (right)

*Location: **RMS Olympic (at sea) / Paris***

## April 3 — Friday

*(printed: D 7h. 42m., aft.)*

> Olympic
> landed 11 p. m.

## April 4 — Saturday

> Paris 5.30 a. m.
> Schlumberg[er]
> Seligmann

**Places:** Olympic, Paris  
**People:** Schlumberger[?], Seligmann  

**Transcriber's note:** Apr 3: landed at 11 p.m. (off the Olympic, presumably at Cherbourg/Southampton). Apr 4: reached Paris at 5.30 a.m.; saw Schlumberger and the dealer Seligmann. Printed sub-note under Apr 3 is a moon-phase time; faint bleed-through reads 'Palm Sunday' (ignored as it belongs to other side).
